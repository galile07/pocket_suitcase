Version 3.x Core Archetype Glossary

☀ Lingering Glow T0 Apex

Evolution of the Burn mechanic. Enemy Burn/Halo stacks are converted into Lingering Glow, triggering massive refracted follow-up beams and defense ignore when detonated by Beryl, Lorentz Butterfly, Isolde [E1], and Marsha.

⚡ Dynamo Electric Array T0 Apex

Nicknamed the "Dynamo Wheelchair." Infuses the battlefield with a Pulsing Electric Field (Lv 1–3) via Brume, Charon, Ulrich, and Enigma, granting AP+1, incantation shuffling, and surging electromagnetic bursts.

🔗 Conduit Resonance T0 Apex

Links allied and enemy nodes together so single-target incantations echo across linked targets with multiplied Crit DMG. Anchored by The Twins, Coppélia, Hedone, and Everecho.

🩸 Bloodtithe Domain T0.5 Meta

High-risk, high-reward HP-fluctuation pool. Allies sacrifice HP into a shared Bloodtithe Pool via Semmelweis [E1] and Rubuska so Nautika [E1] and Sentinel can unleash devastating crimson finishers.

🎼 Impromptu Incantation T0.5 Meta

Consumes allied Eureka to build Inspiration points, automatically firing multi-hit musical barrages at the end of the round. Core crew: Barcarola, Paper Heron / Aleph, Matilda [E2] / Regulus [E1], and Kiperina.

🎯 Bullet & Ultimate Artillery T1 Strong

Combines special Bullet arrays from Lopera [E1] with rapid Moxie battery support from Moldir [E1] and Narcissus to fuel relentless Ultimate salvos from Igor or Recoleta.

• Unlock Requirement: 6★ Arcanists require Insight III Level 30 (5★ units require Insight III Level 1). Unlocking the base Euphoria node grants massive flat stats and unlocks the Epiphany skill tree.

• Epiphany Ranks I–IV: Using Keys of Thought, Sprouts of Fantasy, and Seeds of Inspiration (farmed weekly via Trance in Reveries in the Rain), you awaken 4 sequential passive upgrades that completely modernize the unit's kit (e.g., Isolde E1 enabling Lingering Glow, Semmelweis E1 supercharging Bloodtithe, or Matilda E2 granting huge Impromptu Crit conversion).

• Multiple Euphoria Paths (E1 vs E2): Certain Arcanists have two distinct Euphoria specializations—always check the Character Build Modal before spending Keys of Thought!

• Once at Resonance 10: You unlock Resonance Pattern Polarization (using Sinister Shadow / Crystal Casket materials). This shifts your character's base Resonance frame toward specialized stat distributions (such as Placidity, Genuineness, or Stupor) to maximize Crit Rate, Attack, or HP.

• Resonance 10 vs 15: Keep Supports and Sustainers at Resonance 9 or 10. Only push Resonance 13–15 on your favorite T0 Main Carries (Beryl, Brume, The Twins, Nautika) once you have surplus Caskets from Mane's Bulletin and Reveries.

• Never buy duplicate 6★ Psychubes from the shop just to amplify! Event signature Psychubes are only sold once; always buy Gluttony using Thoughts in Entirety (Pneuma Shop) or Mane's Bulletin currency.

• Top Amplification Targets (A1 → A5): Prioritize universal or hypercarry Psychubes like Refracted Prism (Lingering Glow), Surge of the Dynamo (Dynamo), Cracks in the Shell (Extra Action Support), and The Third Commitment (Array Support).
